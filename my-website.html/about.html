About larper.ai

Building the future of intelligent systems, one agent at a time.

// Our Mission

larper.ai started as an excuse to stop overthinking and start building with AI. In a space filled with noise and grand promises, we focus on what matters: creating useful, reliable systems that genuinely solve problems.

We believe the future isn't about software that sits idle. It's about intelligent agents that can reason, act, and adapt. Systems that work while you sleep, learn from experience, and scale with your ambitions.

We're early. We're still figuring things out. Some experiments will work, others won't. That's the process. Build, learn, iterate, and improve—one useful project at a time.

Founders

Meet the Team

The minds behind larper.ai

JB

John Boyles

Co-founder

John isn't an AI expert by traditional definitions. He's someone deeply curious about what these systems can actually do, and motivated enough to put in the work to find out.

His approach is straightforward: learn by doing, test ideas in the real world, and build things that solve genuine problems. No gatekeeping, no pretense. Just consistent effort and a genuine interest in understanding how AI can transform the way we work.

Most of this is learning in real time—testing ideas, seeing what breaks, and trying to get a little better every day. The goal isn't perfection. It's progress.

MS

Mayank Sharma

Co-founder

Mayank brings a builder's mindset to larper.ai. With a passion for turning complex ideas into functional systems, he focuses on the architecture and implementation that make intelligent agents actually work.

His philosophy centers on practical application over theoretical perfection. Every system should solve a real problem, and every line of code should serve a purpose. He's not interested in building for the sake of building—he wants to create tools that genuinely improve how people work.

When not building, Mayank is exploring emerging AI capabilities, contributing to open source, and thinking about the next generation of intelligent systems. The future of AI isn't abstract to him—it's something he's actively shaping.
